Goldenacre Bowling Club • 1942–2026

Our Club History

A story through the decades, from the club’s beginnings to the present day.

A work in progress

Our story is being gathered

Goldenacre Bowling Club was established in 1942. We’re collecting memories, photographs and records to tell the fuller story of the people and events that have shaped the club.

More details will be added as they become available.

1942 — Club Founded

The origins of our home on the green.

  • Goldenacre Bowling Club is officially established amidst World War II, forming a vital sporting hub for the local Edinburgh community.
1943–1959

The post-war growth years.

  • Early members laid down the physical foundations of our green, clubhouse, and early tournament fixtures within Edinburgh.
1960–1979

Expanding the fixture cards and membership.

  • Club invitationals grew in prominence, bringing elite bowlers from across Midlothian to Goldenacre. Refurbishments to club amenities expanded our base.
1980–1999

Strong regional representations.

  • Goldenacre teams regularly surged up the local district tables. Internal club championships saw highly competitive battles on our home green.
2000–2009 — The Golden Year of 2006

A historic era for Goldenacre, defined by our highest-ever individual national victory and elite city-wide singles titles.

  • 2006 — Scottish National Gents Singles Champion: In the club's ultimate men's national triumph, Billy Mellors proudly represented Goldenacre to win the prestigious Bowls Scotland National Gents Singles Championship, defeating Northfield's Craig England in the final. Billy stands as the club's sole Gents National Singles Champion at the highest tier of Scottish bowls.
  • 2006 — EBA Tait Trophy Winner: At just 21 years old, Colin Hutchison captured the historic Edinburgh Bowling Association (EBA) Tait Trophy. By winning the oldest single-handed bowling trophy in Scotland, Colin cemented his place in Edinburgh bowling history.
  • 2004 — Victor Paris Singles Champion: Setting the stage for his decade of dominance, Colin Hutchison claimed his first Victor Paris Knockout Singles crown, showcasing Goldenacre's elite individual pedigree across the city.
2010–2019

A golden decade defined by major league championships, team triumphs, and exceptional individual club paths.

  • 2019 — EBL Division 3 Champions: The first team squad—captained by vital contributions from skips Chris Fallon and Ewan Fallon—captured the Edinburgh Bowling League Division 3 Championship title.
  • 2019 — Tait Trophy Finalist: Following a brilliant run in the local pathways, Gents Club Champion Ewan Fallon proudly represented Goldenacre all the way to the EBA Tait Trophy Final at Parkside.
  • 2019 — Bowls Scotland Over 55s Regional Champion: Jim Hutchison secured a monumental regional singles title, booking his place to fly the Goldenacre flag at the Senior National Finals on the #RoadToAyr.
  • 2018 — Multi-Time Singles Kingpin: Colin Hutchison secured his second historic Victor Paris Knockout Singles Championship title, cementing his legacy as a 6-time club champion.
  • 2019 — EWBA Fours Champions: The ladies' team of Jean Summers, Sandra Kennedy, Margaret Elder, and Lorna Mackay captured the Edinburgh Women's Bowling Association (EWBA) Fours title.
  • 2019 — District 14 Excellence: Jean Summers, Rita Reid, and Carol Hogg claimed the District 14 Ladies Triples Championship.
  • 2019 — Club Championship Milestones: Ewan Fallon secured the Gents Club Championship in a memorable final against Colin Hutchison, alongside Paul McLaren (2-Bowl Champion) and David Barclay (Gerrard Handicap Champion).
2020–2026

Recent history has seen Goldenacre excel on the local stage, send multi-time champions back to the national stage, and step into the spotlight as a regional host.

  • 2026 — EBA County Championship Pairs Winners: Gary Robertson and Malcolm Thomson brought major silverware to the club by capturing the Edinburgh Bowling Association (EBA) Pairs title.
  • 2026 — Steele Trophy Winner: Rita Reid added to the club's trophy cabinet by winning the regional Steele Trophy final.
    • 2025 - EBA set singles winnderGary Robertson won the EBA set singles bring home his first major silverware to the club.
  • 2024 — Bowls Scotland Over 55s Regional Champion (Two-Time Winner): Jim Hutchison triumphed yet again in a brilliant regional singles campaign, defeating Dudley Bowling Club's G. Flight to secure his second modern pathway qualification to the national finals at Ayr.
  • 2022 — National Host Venue: Goldenacre was selected by Bowls Scotland as the official host venue for the high-profile Ladies National Top 10 East Region Finals.
