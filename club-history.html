Honours & Heritage

Over Eight Decades of Bowling Excellence

Founded in 1942, Goldenacre Bowling Club has built a rich tradition on and off the green. From national titles and historic city trophies to regional league triumphs, explore the milestones and players that have shaped our club history.

🏆 Club Crown Jewels

National Singles Triumph (2006)

Billy Mellors claimed Goldenacre's highest-ever individual national title, winning the Bowls Scotland National Gents Singles Championship.

Historic Tait Trophy (2006)

At just 21 years old, Colin Hutchison won Scotland's oldest single-handed bowling trophy, leading into elite county and international representation.

County & Regional Dominance

From Gary Robertson's 2025 EBA Set Singles and 2026 Pairs titles to Jim Hutchison's dual Over-55 Regional crowns on the #RoadToAyr.

Decade-by-Decade Timeline

2020–2026 — Recent Honours & Hosting Spotlight

Recent history has seen Goldenacre excel on the local stage, send multi-time champions back to the national stage, and step into the spotlight as a regional host.

  • 2026 — EBA County Championship Pairs Winners: Gary Robertson and Malcolm Thomson brought major county silverware to the club by capturing the Edinburgh Bowling Association (EBA) Pairs title.
  • 2025 — EBA Set Singles Champion: Gary Robertson added an outstanding individual Edinburgh title to the club's honors by winning the EBA Set Singles Championship.
  • 2023 — EBA Top 10 Finalists: Goldenacre put up a phenomenal team fight in the Edinburgh Top 10 Championship Final at Gorgie Mills against Slateford, narrowly missing out on the title by just a couple of shots.
  • 2026 — Steele Trophy Winner: Rita Reid added to the club's trophy cabinet by winning the regional Steele Trophy final.
  • 2024 — Bowls Scotland Over 55s Regional Champion (Two-Time Winner): Jim Hutchison triumphed yet again in a brilliant regional singles campaign, defeating Dudley Bowling Club's G. Flight to secure his second modern pathway qualification to the national finals at Ayr.
  • 2022 — National Host Venue: Goldenacre was selected by Bowls Scotland as the official host venue for the high-profile Ladies National Top 10 East Region Finals.

2010–2019 — League Championships & Individual Success

A golden decade defined by major league championships, team triumphs, and exceptional individual club paths.

  • 2019 — EBL Division 3 Champions: The first team squad—captained by vital skip leadership from Chris Fallon and Ewan Fallon—captured the Edinburgh Bowling League Division 3 Championship title.
  • 2019 — Tait Trophy Finalist: Following a brilliant run in the local pathways, Gents Club Champion Ewan Fallon proudly represented Goldenacre all the way to the EBA Tait Trophy Final at Parkside.
  • 2019 — Bowls Scotland Over 55s Regional Champion: Jim Hutchison secured a monumental regional singles title, booking his place to fly the Goldenacre flag at the Senior National Finals on the #RoadToAyr.
  • 2018 — Multi-Time Singles Kingpin: Multiple club championship titles were secured, cementing a legacy of 6-time club championship dominance.
  • 2019 — EWBA Fours Champions: The ladies' team of Jean Summers, Sandra Kennedy, Margaret Elder, and Lorna Mackay captured the Edinburgh Women's Bowling Association (EWBA) Fours title.
  • 2019 — District 14 Excellence: Jean Summers, Rita Reid, and Carol Hogg claimed the District 14 Ladies Triples Championship.
  • 2019 — Club Championship Milestones: Ewan Fallon secured the Gents Club Championship in a memorable final against fellow club leader Colin Hutchison, alongside Paul McLaren capturing the 2-Bowl Championship and David Barclay taking the Gerrard Handicap.

2000–2009 — The Golden Era & National Glory

A historic era for Goldenacre, defined by our highest-ever individual national victory and elite city-wide singles titles.

  • 2006 — Scottish National Gents Singles Champion: In the club's ultimate men's national triumph, Billy Mellors proudly represented Goldenacre to win the prestigious Bowls Scotland National Gents Singles Championship, defeating Northfield's Craig England in the final. Billy stands as the club's sole Gents National Singles Champion at the highest tier of Scottish bowls.
  • 2006 — EBA Tait Trophy Winner & County/International Pathways: At just 21 years old, Colin Hutchison captured the historic Edinburgh Bowling Association (EBA) Tait Trophy, cementing his place in Edinburgh bowling history. Alongside city-wide titles and multi-time club championships, Colin achieved county and international recognition by representing the Scottish Under-25 and indoor international teams, later contributing to the sport nationally as Bowls Scotland's National Development Manager.
  • 2004 — Victor Paris Singles Champion: Setting the stage for a decade of dominance, the city-wide crown was claimed via the Victor Paris Knockout Singles, showcasing Goldenacre's elite individual pedigree across Edinburgh.

1942–1959 — Foundation Years

The origins of our home on the green.

  • 1942 — Club Founded: Goldenacre Bowling Club is officially established amidst World War II, forming a vital sporting hub for the local Edinburgh community.
  • 1943–1959 — Post-War Growth: Early members laid down the physical foundations of our green, clubhouse, and early tournament fixtures within Edinburgh.

1960–1999 — Regional Expansion

Expanding the fixture cards, membership, and district presence.

  • 1960–1979 — Invitational Prominence: Club invitationals grew, bringing elite bowlers from across Midlothian to Goldenacre. Refurbishments expanded amenities.
  • 1980–1999 — District Progress: Goldenacre teams regularly surged up the local district tables with fierce internal club championships.
