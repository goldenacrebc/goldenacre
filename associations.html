Associations

The organisations supporting bowls in Edinburgh and across Scotland.

Edinburgh Bowling Association

Edinburgh Bowling Association was established in 2020, following the merger of the Edinburgh & Leith Bowling Association and the Edinburgh Bowling League. It provides league and individual competitions for clubs in Bowls Scotland Districts 14 and 15, bringing Edinburgh clubs together and helping their members enjoy and develop in the sport.

Learn more at Edinburgh Bowling Association

Bowls Scotland

Founded in 2011, Bowls Scotland is the national governing body for lawn bowls in Scotland. It supports clubs and competitions across the country, and works to develop the sport while making it welcoming and accessible to people of all ages and abilities.
