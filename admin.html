Committee Publishing Guide

Tips for publishing updates effectively on goldenacre.co.uk.

  • Team Sheets: Select team-sheets.json every Friday night, choose 1st or 2nd XVI, fill in the opponent, venue, and rink player names, and hit publish.
  • Club News: Posts directly to the home page under Internal News or External Competitions.
  • Social Events: Automatically updates the social calendar feed for social roll-ups and events.
  • Update Delay: Please allow 1 to 2 minutes for GitHub Pages to rebuild and render updates across browsers.
