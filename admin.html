Committee Publishing Guide

Tips for publishing updates effectively on goldenacre.co.uk.

  • Team Sheets: Select the Team Sheets card every Friday night, fill out the rinks, and hit publish.
  • Internal/External News: Posts directly to the home page under respective news feeds.
  • Social Events, Roll-ups & Coaching: Categorize events properly and add an optional gold pill tag badge.
  • Archivist & Restorer: Check articles to archive them or uncheck previously archived articles to restore them to live status instantly.
  • Calendar Manager: Add future fixtures, edit event details, or remove test entries directly from `events.json`.
  • Broadcast Alert Manager & Sponsors Manager: Manage footer commercial sponsors and urgent notices easily.
  • Update Delay: Please allow 1 to 2 minutes for GitHub Pages to rebuild and render updates across browsers.
