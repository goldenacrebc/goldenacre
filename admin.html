Committee Publishing Guide

Tips for publishing updates effectively on goldenacre.co.uk.

  • Club News: Posts directly to the home page under Internal News or External Competitions[cite: 5].
  • Social Events: Automatically updates the social calendar feed for social roll-ups, country dancing, and winter pool[cite: 5].
  • Image Uploads: Photos are saved directly into the repository's photos/ folder and optimized for mobile devices.
  • Update Delay: Please allow 1 to 2 minutes for GitHub Pages to rebuild and render updates across browsers.
