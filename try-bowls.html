Try Bowls at Goldenacre

Easy to try, easy to enjoy

Whether you’re completely new to bowls or just curious to give it another go, you’ll find a relaxed welcome at our club in Warriston, Edinburgh. No previous experience is needed.

We can provide friendly introductory coaching and equipment to borrow, so you can focus on enjoying your first time on the green.
