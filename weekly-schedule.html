Weekly Schedule

Regular social events at Goldenacre Bowling Club.

Monday Monday Night Pool League Bar open Social 7:00 PM sharp

Every second Monday, starting 12 October. Pool players of all abilities are welcome.

Tuesday Country Dancing Social 2:00 PM

Join us every Tuesday afternoon for country dancing. Everyone is welcome.

Friday Friday Night Social Bar open Social From 6:30 PM

Everyone is welcome to come along for a drink, a catch-up and a chance to practise your pool skills over the winter months. A good laugh is always guaranteed.
