TaxAssist Accountants (Goldenacre)

Founded nationally in 1995 to provide accessible, dedicated financial support to small-and-medium businesses, sole traders, landlords, and personal taxpayers, TaxAssist Accountants has grown into the UK's largest small business accounting network. Operating locally at 18 Montagu Terrace in Goldenacre, their team offers expert, fixed-fee accounting, tax planning, bookkeeping, and payroll services tailored to independent businesses and residents across Edinburgh.

Blackhall Moving & Storage

Established by owner Simon Munro and built on a foundation of family removals experience, Blackhall Moving & Storage is one of Edinburgh’s leading local relocation and storage providers. Backed by over 30 years of industry expertise, they deliver professional home removals, office relocations, full packing assistance, and secure storage solutions across Edinburgh, the Lothians, and throughout the UK.
