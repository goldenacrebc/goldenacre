About the Club & Facilities

Established in 1942, Goldenacre Bowling Club is based in Warriston, Edinburgh. We welcome bowlers of all ages and abilities, whether you are new to the sport, enjoy playing socially or want to represent the club in competitions and league matches.

Our friendly club has an active social calendar throughout the year, and new members are always welcome. If you are new why not come along to one of our Try Bowls sessions.

Membership & Affiliations

We offer Gents, Ladies and Junior memberships. The club is affiliated to Bowls Scotland, Edinburgh Bowling Association and Edinburgh Top Ten.

Club Facilities

Our facilities include an outdoor bowling green for league matches, club competitions and social roll-ups; a clubhouse pool table with a fortnightly winter league; and a function hall for up to 80 people, available to hire for private events.

Please note: We do not provide catering or accept bookings for 18th or 21st birthday parties.

Upcoming Events

When the green closes for winter, join us at the club for our fortnightly pool league, weekly country dancing, and social evenings. Everyone is welcome.
