Goldenacre Bowling Club

About the Club & Facilities

Find us in Warriston, Edinburgh. Select the map to open directions.

Established in 1942, Goldenacre Bowling Club is based in Warriston, Edinburgh. We welcome bowlers of all ages and abilities, whether you are new to the sport, enjoy playing socially or want to represent the club in competitions and league matches. Our friendly club has an active social calendar throughout the year, and new members are always welcome.

Membership & affiliations

We offer Gents, Ladies and Junior memberships. The club is affiliated to Bowls Scotland, Edinburgh Bowling Association and Edinburgh Top Ten.

Club facilities

Enjoy our outdoor green for competitive league matches, club competitions and social roll-ups.

Our clubhouse pool table hosts a fortnightly Monday night pool league during the winter. Everyone is welcome.

The function hall accommodates up to 80 people and is available to hire for private events. Bar facilities are available on request. To check hall availability, contact us.

Please note: We do not provide catering or accept bookings for 18th or 21st birthday parties.
